# Ethereal Obsidian Design System

## Overview
A sophisticated developer portfolio combining senior-level professionalism with subtle, tasteful Harry Potter-inspired magical touches: ambient Lumos luminescence, floating golden embers, glassmorphism, and ethereal apparition smoke transitions between sections.

## Colors & Atmosphere
- Deep obsidian canvas (`#07080d`) providing high contrast for clean technical presentation.
- Glass surfaces (`#151926`) with 1px border glows reflecting ambient light.
- Warm golden Lumos highlights (`#d4af37`, `#ffd778`) on interactive focus and active states.

## Typography
- Headings: Refined classical serif (`Cinzel`) used with modern restraint.
- Body, UI & Code: Ultra-legible sans-serif (`Inter`) ensuring crystal-clear readability for technical recruiters and clients.

## Transitions
- Section changes trigger a golden Lumos wand shockwave accompanied by sparkling starlight glints, paired with fluid Framer Motion view cross-dissolves.
