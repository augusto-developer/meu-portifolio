# Bibliotheca Magica — Gryffindor Atelier Design System

## Vision & Atmosphere
A warm, atmospheric magical library interface inspired by the noble Gryffindor heritage:
- Deep mahogany wood canvases (`#0d090b`, `#170f13`).
- Rich royal scarlet and burgundy wine accents (`#801b2b`, `#4a0e18`).
- Burnished brass bookplate trims and filigree corner rivets (`#d4a738`).
- Warm candlelight glow and floating motes.
- Bookplate folio framing, classical serif headings, and ultra-readable parchment-toned typography.
